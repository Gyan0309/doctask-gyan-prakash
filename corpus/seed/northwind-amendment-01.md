# AMENDMENT NO. 1

**To:** Master Services Agreement NW-MSA-2024-0301
**Between:** Meridian Retail Group and Northwind Analytics LLC
**Amendment Date:** 2025-06-15
**Effective Date:** 2025-07-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This Amendment No. 1 amends the Master Services Agreement dated 2024-03-01. Except as
expressly amended below, all terms of the Agreement remain in full force and effect.

## 2. Amended Fees

Section 2 (Fees) is deleted and replaced in its entirety. With effect from 2025-07-01,
the hourly rate is $195 per hour for all standard analytics services.

## 3. Amended Payment Terms

Section 3 (Invoicing and Payment) is amended so that, with effect from 2025-07-01,
payment terms are net 30 days from the date of a correctly rendered invoice.

## 4. Precedence

Where any term of this Amendment conflicts with the Agreement, this Amendment prevails
with effect from the Effective Date stated above.

**Signed:** 2025-06-15
