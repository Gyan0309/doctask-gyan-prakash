# AMENDMENT NO. 2

**To:** Master Services Agreement NW-MSA-2024-0301
**Between:** Meridian Retail Group and Northwind Analytics LLC
**Amendment Date:** 2025-11-20
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This Amendment No. 2 amends the Master Services Agreement dated 2024-03-01, as
previously amended by Amendment No. 1 dated 2025-06-15.

## 2. Amended Limitation of Liability

Section 6 (Limitation of Liability) is amended so that, with effect from 2026-01-01,
the liability cap is $2,500,000 in aggregate for all claims arising under or in
connection with the Agreement.

## 3. Amended Termination Notice

Section 5 (Termination) is amended so that, with effect from 2026-01-01, either party
may terminate for convenience by giving 90 days written notice.

## 4. No Other Changes

The hourly rate, payment terms and renewal provisions are unchanged by this Amendment.

**Signed:** 2025-11-20
