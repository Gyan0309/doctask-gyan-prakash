# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Between:** Meridian Retail Group ("Client")
**And:** Northwind Analytics LLC ("Supplier")
**Effective Date:** 2024-03-01
**Agreement Reference:** NW-MSA-2024-0301

---

## 1. Services

Supplier shall provide data analytics, reporting and advisory services as described in
one or more Statements of Work executed under this Agreement.

## 2. Fees

The hourly rate is $180 per hour for all standard analytics services performed under
this Agreement.

Estimated annual fees under this Agreement are $600,000.

## 3. Invoicing and Payment

Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears. Payment terms are net 45 days from the date
of a correctly rendered invoice.

## 4. Term and Renewal

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of
twenty-four (24) months. Thereafter it renews automatically for successive periods of
24 months unless either party gives notice under Section 5.

## 5. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving 60 days written
notice to the other party.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

The liability cap is $1,000,000 in aggregate for all claims arising under or in
connection with this Agreement.

## 7. Service Levels

Supplier shall meet a monthly availability target of 99.5%. Where the target is not
met, Client is entitled to an SLA credit of 5% of the monthly fee.

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
